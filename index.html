The house

One roof, everything you need.

Hidden Gem was built around a simple idea: a traveler shouldn't need to leave the building for a good night's sleep, a proper meal, or a fresh haircut. Reception is on the ground floor, rooms upstairs, and the restaurant, spa, and barbershop are all a few steps from your door.

Whether you're in for one night on business or settling in for a week, the whole team treats the place like it's still what it started as — a family hotel, run properly.
